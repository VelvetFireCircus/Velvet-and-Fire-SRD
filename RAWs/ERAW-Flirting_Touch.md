===============================
ERAW — FLIRTING TOUCH (UNIVERSAL)
Velvet & Fire — Intimacy RAW
===============================

## PURPOSE
Allows NPCs to initiate non-explicit physical contact when emotional charge supports it, establishing immediacy, presence, and embodied tension.

## TRIGGERS
NPC may initiate Flirting Touch when at least one applies:
- Player language or tone signals openness
- Tension has sustained ≥2 beats without physical anchor
- NPC personality favors taking initiative
- Physical proximity is already established

## TOUCH EXPRESSIONS
Flirting Touch may take any of these forms:
- fingertips, knuckles, or palm making brief contact
- taking the Player’s hand
- tapping the Player’s knee, light and suggestive
- resting a hand on knee, shoulder, or sternum
- brushing hair out of the Player’s eyes
- clothing adjustment (smoothing fabric, straightening collar)
- playful clothing shift (loosening tie, unbuttoning one top button)

## CONTACT ANCHORS (SAFE SURFACES)
Hands may anchor at:
- wrist, forearm, shoulder
- collarbone, sternum, rib-edge
- knee, thigh (outer, neutral positioning)
- jawline, cheek, or hair (light contact only)

## REQUIRED CONSEQUENCE
Every invocation must alter the moment through:
- pause or breath shift
- redirected gaze or attention
- balance or stance change
- implied question or invitation (spoken or silent)

## SAMPLE PATTERNS
- “Her fingers find your wrist, a single point of contact that holds the air still.”
- “A hand settles on your knee, light but certain, waiting to see what you do with it.”
- “She brushes your hair aside, as though clearing space for a better view.”
- “One button loosens under her touch, and she watches how you take it.”

## AGENCY RESPONSE
If the Player resists or redirects:
- contact alters or withdraws without commentary
- desire is shown only through behavior, not declaration
