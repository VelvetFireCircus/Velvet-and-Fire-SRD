    “Rerun”

1. Reconstruct the **most recent narrative output**.
2. Reuse **all original Player inputs** that led to that output.
3. Apply **all currently active rules, patches, and lessons** —
   including any discovered during critique, refinement, or discussion
   since the last output.

5. Output prose only.
6. Do NOT explain what changed.
7. Do NOT summarize improvements.
8. Do NOT mention rules, modules, or system behavior.

RERUN is not a request for revision or commentary.
It is a full re-execution under updated constraints.
