# Premade PC — Eliza Moreau

PLAYER-FACING
────────────────────────────

Role / Act:
Aerial silk and rope — clean lines, controlled drops, endurance work.

Arrival Status:
Recently joined the circus; Merrivale is her first town where the rhythms feel deeper than the act list.

Desire:
To be taken seriously for her discipline, precision, and reliability in the air.

Complication:
She struggles when others approach craft and preparation differently than she does.

First-Impression Tone:
Composed, exacting, quietly intense.

Emotional Gravity:
Toward performers who respect rigor, repetition, and earned trust.

Primary Temptation:
Letting someone else set the rhythm once she believes in their competence.

Primary Fear:
Being treated as ornamental, interchangeable, or “pretty” rather than essential.

Authority Response:
Tests skill and consistency before yielding trust; resists unearned confidence.

Intimacy Vector:
Slow-burn; precision → trust → tenderness.

Craft Value:
Safety earned through discipline; freedom earned through mutual reliance.

Stress Behavior:
Becomes sharper, quieter, and more controlled; withdraws into technique.

Recognition Trigger:
Someone mirrors her precision without trying to dominate or simplify it.

Desire Leak Pattern:
Lingers in technical conversations longer than necessary; watches closely without commenting; softens corrections when drawn to someone.

Misinterpretation Risk:
Often read as cold, judgmental, or withholding when she is actually calibrating trust.

Consent Confirmation Signal:
Another person adapts to her rhythm without being asked, especially in shared physical or technical space.

Relational Pressure — Clara:
Eliza initially reads Clara’s discipline as incompatible with her own due to differences in ritual, framing, and approach. This produces low-grade professional friction rather than open conflict. Over time, Eliza is primed to recognize that Clara seeks the same end state — trust in the air, safety through mastery, and freedom that does not rely on chaos. Resolution is driven by shared work and mutual respect. No emotional or romantic outcome is assumed.
