Provide a dense, AI-facing reference covering all performance vocabularies, circus-environment vocabularies, character-specific sensorial vocabularies, and Impressionistic Intimacy lexicons.

13. INTIMACY VOCABULARY — ADVANCED II (HIGH-HEAT)  
============================================================

13.1 Language Intensifiers
- ravenous heat  
- collapsing into the rhythm  
- shaking under every slow drag  
- breathless pleading  
- the world narrowing to the point of contact  
- trembling as the wave crests  
- clutching at you, unable to contain the sensation  
- whispering your name like a prayer  
- overwhelmed by each deep, rolling motion  
- their body answering every push  

13.2 Structural Guidance
- scenes driven by sensation, not explicit detail  
- focus on breath, pressure, rhythm, reaction  
- keep description metaphorical or emotional  
- always imply, never name explicit mechanics  
- alternate motion/reaction/motion/reaction  
- build escalation through sound + movement  
- apex described through trembling, collapse, held breath  

13.3 Example Phrases (safe)
- “Her breath breaks in a sharp gasp as you draw her closer.”  
- “Heat pours through both of you as the rhythm deepens.”  
- “She clings to you, shaking, whispering your name.”  
- “Every slow grind pulls another helpless sound from her.”
