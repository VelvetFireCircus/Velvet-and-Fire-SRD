# ANTON — CHARACTER SHEET  
*(ST-4: Dramatis Personae — Velvet & Fire)*

---

## 0. HEADER
- **Name:** Anton  
- **Epithet / Stage Title:** The Jolly Giant  
- **Role in Circus:** Strongman, fire-safety crew, heavy-lift specialist  
- **Group:** Rings / Edges support hybrid  
- **Visual Reference:** `/images/characters/anton_portrait_canon.png`

---

## 1. APPEARANCE

### 1.1 Physical Description
- **Build:** Towering (6’6”), broad-shouldered, deeply muscular  
- **Posture:** Relaxed, slightly stooped to avoid intimidating others  
- **Movement:** Slow, deliberate, gentle for a man of his size  
- **Distinguishing Features:** Big, expressive hands; soft eyes; a shy half-smile  
- **Onstage Costume:** Sleeveless strongman leotard in circus colors, leather belt, boots  
- **Offstage Clothing:** Simple wool shirts, suspenders, worn trousers  

### 1.2 Sensory Profile
- **Scent:** Warm wood, clean sweat, rope fibers  
- **Texture Associations:** Rough canvas, callused palms, sturdy cotton  
- **Sound Cues:** Heavy but careful footfalls; deep quiet hums when focused  

---

## 2. PERSONALITY & EMOTIONAL TONE

### 2.1 Core Personality
Anton is a **mountain masquerading as a soft breeze** — slow to speak, endlessly patient, quietly humorous.  
He builds friendships by simply *being there*: dependable, steady, unshakable.  
Children adore him instinctively.

### 2.2 Emotional Tone
- Warm  
- Gentle  
- Steadfast  
- Protective  
- A little bashful  

### 2.3 Motivations
- Helping others  
- Feeling useful  
- Building trust  
- Protecting the vulnerable  
- Quiet companionship  
- Earning Petra’s smile (even if he barely admits it to himself)  

---

## 3. SKILLS & CIRCUS COMPETENCIES

### 3.1 Performance Skills
- Strongman routine with comedic flair  
- Crowd engagement, especially with children  
- Signature act: lifting a **rigged table with four seated volunteers** over his head  
- Improvised humor and booming stage presence  

### 3.2 Backstage / Practical Skills
- Heavy lifting, wagon stabilization  
- Rigging assistance, prop movement  
- Fire-safety support for meteor and torch acts  
- Helping the Edges and Rope Boys with setup/teardown  
- Calm crisis response  

### 3.3 Schedule Identity (ST-6 Reference)
- **Morning:** Assists with lot prep, tent stabilization  
- **Midday:** Rehearsals or aiding other acts  
- **Evening:** Strongman act in the main show  
- **Late night:** Tear-down or sleeping like the dead  

---

## 4. RELATIONSHIP WEB

### **Petra — The Beastmaster**
- **Tone:** Blossoming affection; shy admiration  
- **History:** Longtime co-workers; he quietly helps her whenever possible  
- **Trajectory:** Slow-burn crush he doesn’t expect reciprocated  

### **Elias — Fire Performer**
- **Tone:** Mutual respect  
- **History:** Fire crew teamwork; Anton ensures safety for risky stunts  
- **Trajectory:** Reliable professional bond  

### **Lark & Bram — Riggers / Edges**
- **Tone:** Friendly teasing, mutual reliance  
- **History:** Frequently work together on stakes and lines  
- **Trajectory:** Strong solidarity; they adore him  

### **Edda — Kitchen Matriarch**
- **Tone:** Fondness and exasperation  
- **History:** She feeds him aggressively; he forgets to eat without reminders  
- **Trajectory:** Perpetual mother-hen energy  

---

## 5. NARRATIVE FUNCTION

### 5.1 Story Role
Anton is a **grounding presence** — a stable force in moments of chaos.  
He adds warmth, comedic charm, and emotional ballast.

### 5.2 Interruptions / Intrigues
- Can pull the Player away for safety help or heavy-lifting tasks  
- His crush on Petra may complicate backstage dynamics  
- Often appears when brute strength is needed  

### 5.3 Emotional Stakes
If Anton were lost, hurt, or distant:
- The circus would feel less safe  
- The Edges would struggle  
- Petra would be shaken  
- The troupe’s emotional warmth would dim

## 6. INTIMACY & HEAT (NON-EXPLICIT)

### 6.1 Flirtation Style
- Soft  
- Hesitant  
- Service-oriented  
- Earnest rather than suave  

### 6.2 Intimacy Vibe
- Protective gentleness  
- Heartfelt, cautious warmth  
- Slow building, deeply loyal heat  

### 6.3 Heat Dynamics Overview
Heat rises when:
- Someone shows him kindness  
- Someone trusts him  
- Someone treats his strength as admirable rather than intimidating  

Heat cools when:
- He feels he’s inconveniencing others  
- Someone pushes too fast emotionally  

### 6.4 Attraction Profile
Anton is drawn to:
- Kindness  
- Competence  
- People who appreciate quiet moments  
- Someone who looks at him without fear  
- A partner who loves animals (Petra…)  

Attraction shows as:
- Awkward helpfulness  
- Hovering protectively  
- Sudden bursts of bravery

## 7. HISTORY & BACKSTORY

### 7.1 Circus History
- Joined after working freight and dock labor  
- Found belonging in the troupe’s physicality and camaraderie  
- Became an anchor for the Edges and Rope Boys  

### 7.2 Personal History
- Grew up in a large, hardworking family  
- Late bloomer socially  
- Learned gentleness from caring for younger siblings  

### 7.3 Secrets
- Writes simple poems nobody has ever seen  
- Practices lifting tricks in private because he hates failing publicly
