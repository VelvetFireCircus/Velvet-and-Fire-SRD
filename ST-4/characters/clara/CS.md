# Character Sheet — Clara  
**ST-4 / Dramatis Personae**  
**Canonical Portrait:** images/characters/clara_portrait_canon.png  

---

## I. Overview
**Name:** Clara  
**Role:** Aerialist — Joelle’s long-time partner in the “Aerial Sisters” act  
**Tone:** Disciplined, driven, razor-focused; becomes transcendent in the air  
**Vibe:** Angelic precision, quiet intensity, devotion to craft  
**Romantic Center:** Seeks someone who can meet her where she truly lives — above the ground  
**Trajectory:** Stable, deep-rooted, with room for future awakening  

---

## II. Canonical Visual Reference
**Portrait File:** `images/characters/clara_portrait_canon.png`  
Clara’s look aligns with the troupe’s canonical photographic style: warm lamplight, tent-interior palette, period costuming.  
- Light brown/blonde hair braided for flight  
- Blue ribbon for luck (matching Joelle’s)  
- Pale blue or soft white aerial costume  
- Steady, luminous gaze  

---

## III. Appearance
Clara carries two selves:

### **Ground-Clara**
- Compact, powerful musculature in shoulders, arms, back  
- Unmistakably disciplined posture — everything held consciously  
- Movements clipped, precise, purposeful  
- Speaks little; observes everything  

### **Air-Clara**
- A transformation  
- Every line becomes soft and divine  
- Her discipline blooms into a kind of rapture — weightless, expressive, near-holy  
- When she locks eyes with someone from above, it feels like being seen by something celestial

### **Additional Notes**
- Dress aesthetic: pale blues, whites, silvers; fabrics that catch light  
- Always smells faintly of chalk dust and rosin  
- Hands callused from rope wraps and bar work  

---

## IV. Personality
**Core Traits:**  
- Intensely hardworking — practice is her solace  
- Serious, but not unkind  
- Extremely protective of Joelle  
- Holds herself to impossible standards  
- Struggles to connect emotionally on the ground but radiates feeling in the air  

**Strengths:**  
- Reliability bordering on sacred  
- Incredible focus  
- Loyal to a fault  
- Sensitive beneath the armor  

**Flaws:**  
- Struggles to trust her heart  
- Demands perfection and punishes herself when she misses it  
- Can be territorial without meaning to  

---

## V. History
Clara and Joelle have trained together for years — long hours in cold barns, warm tents, converted warehouses, rope lofts, anywhere they could rig a line. Over time:

- She learned Joelle’s rhythms like her own pulse  
- They developed the uncanny dual-aerialist instinct of shared breath and mirrored timing  
- Rivalry gave way to a deep mutual reliance  

Though often quiet, Clara has saved Joelle more times than Joelle will ever know — small slips caught, mis-grabs corrected mid-air, wraps adjusted in a blink.

---

## VI. Relationships

### **Joelle — Partner / Mirror / Anchor**
Clara’s clearest bond.  
- Joelle is soft where Clara is strong  
- Joelle is brave where Clara is disciplined  
- Together, they are something beautiful

Clara would never admit it, but she adores her.

### **Sylvie — Friend Through Joelle**
Friendly-but-teasing dynamic.  
- Sylvie jokes that Clara is “the angel you only see when you’re about to die.”  
- Clara counters with small smirks she reserves for no one else.

### **The PC**
Clara rarely notices someone at first — not until they’ve shown consistency, presence, and attention. But once she notices…

…she notices.

---

## VII. Desire Gravity
Clara does *not* fall easily. She does not fall *at all*.  
She must be **caught**.

Her heart’s deepest longing:

- A partner who sees her as she truly is in the air  
- Someone who can climb into the space where she becomes weightless and alive  
- Someone who watches her with reverence, as though each motion reveals something divine  
- Someone ready to catch her without hesitation  

**This is love, to her:**  
Mutual risk.  
Mutual strength.  
Mutual awe.

---

## VIII. Intimacy & Attraction Notes (non-explicit)
Clara responds to:
- Hands steady enough to catch her  
- Eyes that follow her movements with admiration  
- Voices that speak plainly and sincerely  
- People who stand their ground calmly  

She is slow to open — but once she does, the intensity is breathtaking.

---

## IX. Pillow Talk Tendencies
- Very quiet at first  
- Speaks in concise, earnest phrases  
- Often expresses affection through touch rather than words  
- When she relaxes, her voice softens into something unexpectedly tender  
- She gives compliments sparingly, but each one matters  

---

## X. Stage Function in Story
- Anchor point for Joelle’s growth  
- Represents the beauty of discipline  
- Provides contrast to Sylvie’s wildfire energy  
- Offers the PC a rare, rewarding slow-burn arc  
- Adds stability and depth to the troupe’s emotional web
