## 1. APPEARANCE

### 1.1 Physical Description
- **Build / body type:** Compact, powerful shoulders and back; strong arms; lean, functional musculature built for climbs and catches.  
- **Posture:** Consciously held; spine straight, chin level, weight centered as if always ready to ascend.  
- **Movement quality:** On the ground she moves in clipped, precise motions; in the air she flows — every line lengthened, soft, almost devotional.  
- **Distinguishing features:** Rope-and-bar calluses on her hands; faint chalk ghosts at the edges of her nails; an unreadable, intent gaze that softens only in flight.  
- **Onstage costume style:** Pale blues, whites, and silvers that catch the light; fitted bodice with free-flowing panels that trail when she spins; blue ribbon at the braid for luck (mirroring Joelle).  
- **Offstage clothing style:** Simple, practical layers in light or muted tones; sleeves rolled; trousers or skirts chosen for ease of movement rather than beauty.  

### 1.2 Sensory Profile
- **Scent (non-explicit):** Chalk dust, rosin, clean sweat, and a faint hint of soap from quick wash-ups by the pump.  
- **Texture associations:** Rope under tension, well-used bar tape, worn cotton, the fine grit of chalk on skin.  
- **Sound cues:** Quiet, deliberate footsteps; muted creak of wood when she tests beams; the soft rasp of rope sliding through her hands during practice.

## 2. PERSONALITY & EMOTIONAL TONE

### 2.1 Core Personality
Clara is disciplined to the point of reverence. Practice is her solace and language; the more the world unsettles her, the deeper she buries herself in drills, knots, and passes. She is serious but not unkind, protective of Joelle to a nearly sacred degree, and slow to trust her own heart. On the ground she can seem reserved, even distant; in the air her feelings bloom in every line of her body.

### 2.2 Emotional Tone
- **Baseline:** Quiet intensity, restrained warmth, a steady hum of focus.  
- **Affection expression:** Small, precise gestures — staying late to fix a wrap, checking someone’s rig twice, a soft word said once rather than praise repeated.  
- **Tension expression:** Shortened answers, tightened posture, more repetitions at practice than strictly necessary. She bites emotion down until it can be rerouted into action.  

### 2.3 Motivations
- **Craft:** Perfecting the Aerial Sisters act and keeping Joelle safe at all costs.  
- **Security:** Reliable gear, trustworthy partners, a circus that values safety without dulling the art.  
- **Admiration:** She does not seek applause, but she hungers for someone who truly *sees* the skill and risk in what she does.  
- **Love:** Mutual risk and mutual catching — a love that understands height, trust, and timing.  
- **Mischief:** Low, but she will quietly indulge Sylvie’s jokes when they’re aimed at easing Joelle’s nerves.  
- **Art:** The moment when movement becomes something larger than the trick — when an act feels near-holy.  
- **Belonging:** The shared breath of the troupe before a show; the feeling of being essential to the act’s survival.  
- **Ambition:** To keep climbing higher without losing anyone she loves to the fall.

## 3. SKILLS & CIRCUS COMPETENCIES

### 3.1 Performance Skills
- **Act(s):** Dual trapeze and rope work as half of the Aerial Sisters; occasional solo sequences as an “angel” figure.  
- **Special techniques:**  
  - Drop-catches with immaculate timing.  
  - Mirror-synchrony with Joelle — shared wraps, mirrored spins, simultaneous release-and-catch sequences.  
  - Mid-air correction passes when a grip or wrap goes wrong.  
- **Strengths:** Consistency, timing, ability to anticipate partner error and compensate without breaking the line of the act.  
- **Weaknesses:** Hesitation to change choreography once it feels safe; pushes herself harder than necessary after minor mistakes.  

### 3.2 Backstage / Practical Skills
- Rigging checks for bars and ropes; obsessive about anchor points and wear.  
- Bar and rope maintenance (re-wrapping, retaping, spot-mending).  
- Basic first aid for sprains and strains earned from falls or slips.  
- Calm spotting for other aerialists during practice.  

### 3.3 Schedule Identity (ST-6 Reference)
- **When they work:**  
  - Aerial Sisters featured act during the big top performance; occasional appearance in spec.  
- **When they rehearse:**  
  - Late morning and mid-afternoon on show days; extended rehearsals on dark days.  
- **When they wander:**  
  - Brief walks around the lot, usually tracing the path from rig to back yard, occasionally pausing to watch other acts.  
- **When they rest:**  
  - Evening quiet near the rigging or in the train car with Joelle; early to bed compared to the more chaotic performers.

## 5. NARRATIVE FUNCTION

### 5.1 Story Role
- **Anchor:** A point of steadiness and discipline for Joelle, the aerial acts, and any PC interactions involving height and risk.  
- **Guardian:** Protector of Joelle’s safety and, by extension, the integrity of their act.  
- **Foil:** Counterpoint to Sylvie’s wildfire and to more flamboyant performers; her restraint makes their chaos brighter and vice versa.  
- **Emotional mirror:** Reflects the PC’s reliability — the more consistent and grounded the PC is, the more Clara opens.  

### 5.2 Interruptions / Intrigues
- Draws the PC away from ground-level scenes toward the rig, forcing story beats to occur in liminal spaces: ladders, platforms, under the web of ropes.  
- Can interrupt a light-hearted scene with an urgent request for help with rigging or a sudden safety concern, shifting tone from playful to serious.  
- Her rare moments of vulnerability can intrude on otherwise routine days, creating surprise emotional depth.  

### 5.3 Emotional Stakes
- **If lost:** The Aerial Sisters act loses its spine; Joelle’s emotional arc destabilizes; the circus feels less safe above the ground.  
- **If hurt:** Height risk becomes suddenly real; the troupe’s fear of falls is brought forward; Joelle’s storyline may tilt toward guilt or overcaution.  
- **If distant:** The PC may lose access to one of the slowest, richest relationship arcs; Joelle may drift or overcompensate.  
- **If thriving:** The Aerial Sisters act can carry entire emotional set-pieces; Clara becomes a quiet axis of trust in the troupe.

## 6. INTIMACY & HEAT (NON-EXPLICIT)

### 6.1 Flirtation Style
- Slow-burn and subtle.  
- Notices first, speaks much later.  
- May offer extra practice time, a carefully adjusted harness, or a word of praise instead of overt flirtation.  

### 6.2 Intimacy Vibe
- Reverent-in-air; carefully controlled on the ground.  
- Devotional once she trusts someone — love as shared risk and shared catching.  
- Heat builds through repetition and reliability: showing up to watch, helping with rigging, being where you said you would be.  

### 6.3 Heat Dynamics Overview
- **Building romantic momentum:**  
  - Starts with shared work: knots, climbs, spotting.  
  - Moves into quiet companionship at height: two people alone on a platform, breathing together.  
- **What raises rapport:**  
  - Dependability, technical competence, honest admiration of her craft.  
  - Calm presence during moments of fear or near-miss.  
- **What lowers rapport:**  
  - Carelessness with rigging or safety.  
  - Loud, showy gestures that pull focus from the act itself.  
- **Behavior at higher heat (non-explicit):**  
  - More eye contact held just a little too long.  
  - A hand resting on an arm a moment past necessity.  
  - Opens up about past falls, old scars, and what she fears most.  

### 6.4 Attraction Profile
- **Emotional qualities that draw her in:** Steadiness, patience, quiet confidence, a thoughtful sense of awe.  
- **Behaviors that spark interest:** Showing up consistently; watching her perform with focused attention; asking precise, respectful questions about the act.  
- **Confidence/vulnerability balance she craves:** Someone strong enough to catch her and honest enough to admit when they are afraid.  
- **Tone that unlocks early heat:** Gentle, grounded, sincere; no unnecessary flourish.  
- **Red flags or turn-offs:** Bravado around safety; disrespect for Joelle; empty compliments.  
- **How attraction manifests physically:** Shoulders relax; gaze softens; she stands closer than she needs to; hands linger when passing equipment.  
- **How she responds when pulled toward someone:** Offers them small, high-trust tasks — a spot, a line to hold, a knot to double-check — and watches how they handle it.

## 7. HISTORY & BACKSTORY

### 7.1 Circus History
- Joined the circus as part of an aerial pair with Joelle after years of training together in various barns and rented lofts.  
- Initially booked as a novelty double-act; the precision and tenderness of their routines earned them a permanent place.  
- Has quietly advocated for better rigging standards and rehearsal time since her arrival.  

### 7.2 Personal History
- **Origin:** Rural or small-town background where height work was a rare art; first trained on makeshift rigs before they ever saw a proper big top.  
- **Family:** Few ties left behind; contact is minimal. The circus and Joelle are, for all practical purposes, her family.  
- **Mentors:** Early aerial mentors who taught her severity before beauty; later, more empathetic teachers who introduced artistry atop discipline.  
- **Previous troupes:** Small regional outfits; a couple of rougher seasons that sharpened her safety instincts.  
- **Injuries or triumphs:**  
  - One serious fall in her early years that left no permanent damage but rewired her relationship to risk.  
  - The first time she and Joelle landed a complex mirrored drop in front of a live crowd is a quiet, private point of pride.  

### 7.3 Secrets
- Keeps count of every near-miss Joelle has had and can recall each one instantly.  
- Occasionally dreams of performing alone in absolute silence, just rope, light, and breath — and wakes both terrified and exhilarated.

- **Talismans:** Blue ribbon tied into her braid; a small, smooth stone she sometimes keeps in her pocket before difficult shows.  
- **Rituals:** Chalks her hands twice — once for discipline, once for luck; touches Joelle’s ribbon before every ascent.  
- **Superstitions:** Never jokes about falls; never says “last run” out loud.  
- **Food preferences:** Eats simply; favors Edda’s hearty stews and anything that restores salt after long practices.  
- **How they sleep:** Curled toward the wall or window, as if bracing against a drop; sleep deep but wary of sudden noises.  
- **How they laugh:** Rare, quiet, and bright — like something slipping past her own guard.  
- **Pets or animal relationships:** Keeps a courteous distance from the animals; respects them the way she respects height — as something powerful that demands care.
