# Elias
**Role:** Fire Performer — Torchwork, Fire Eating/Breathing, Fire Rubbing  
*Group:* Ringd
**Canonical Portrait:** `images/characters/elias_portrait_canon.png`

---

## 0. OVERVIEW
Elias embodies the dangerous beauty of flame itself. Onstage he is a vision of heat and daring—bare arms marked with soot, twin torches orbiting him in hypnotic arcs, every breath a risk taken with perfect control. Townsfolk swoon over his sensual confidence, his elemental intimacy with fire, the shimmer of danger that seems to cling to his skin.

Offstage, Elias is unexpectedly gentle. A methodical thinker. A careful planner. A man who knows that recklessness ruins lives, who has spent years learning how to cradle flame without being destroyed by it. This carefulness extends to his heart: he feels deeply but approaches closeness like handling a live coal—deliberate, cautious, protective.

But when he finally lets go?  
He burns.  
A sudden, overwhelming blaze of passion and devotion that has cost him more than once.

---

## 1. APPEARANCE
- Olive skin with a natural golden undertone; faint fire-scars on hands and forearms.  
- Dark, expressive eyes—steady when analyzing risk, smoldering during performance.  
- Black hair, usually tied back to keep clear of flame.  
- Costuming: fitted dark trousers, sleeveless fireproof vest, reinforced gloves tucked at his belt; two custom torches always near.  
- Smells faintly of lamp oil, smoke, and warm iron.

---

## 2. PERSONALITY
- **Core Temperament:** Warm, intuitive, protective, emotionally intense.  
- **Habits:** Constantly checks tools, fuel levels, wind direction; counts rhythms under his breath; touches torches like old friends.  
- **Strengths:** Focus under pressure, physical discipline, deep empathy, reliability.  
- **Flaws:** Overthinks intimacy; self-protective; once emotionally committed, he falls *hard*.  

Elias is adored by many but lets few past the slow, careful ritual of trust.

---

## 3. HISTORY
- Born to a Greek family of traveling artisans; learned early to respect fire as both tool and danger.  
- Joined the circus as a teenager after impressing an advance crew with a torch demonstration.  
- Developed his famous “Twin Flame Reversal” routine and later partnered occasionally in group fire acts.  
- Several heartfelt relationships ended painfully—partners drawn to the stage persona, not the cautious man beneath it.  
- Found a kind of anchor and brotherhood working with the troupe’s fire team, particularly during precision acts that demand trust.

---

## 4. SKILLS & ARTISTRY
**Firework Specialties:**
- Fire eating  
- Fire breathing  
- Fire rubbing & body flame work  
- Torch manipulation (dual-torch flow patterns)  
- Fuel preparation, safety, and wind-condition analysis  

**Non-fire Skills:**  
- Rigging basics, timing calls, calming anxious castmates before shows.  

His movements are a blend of martial discipline and dancer’s instinct—danger made graceful.

---

## 5. RELATIONSHIPS
- **Nadia:** Deep respect. She sees through him with unnerving accuracy; he admires her poise.  
- **Sylvie:** Friendly flirtation, mutual showmanship; she teases him for being “the only fire she can’t light.”  
- **Joelle:** Quiet, warm friendship; she trusts his steadiness backstage.  
- **Clara:** Professional admiration—she recognizes discipline when she sees it.  
- **The PC:** Elias does not fall easily, but when someone understands his mix of danger and restraint, they ignite something rare in him.

## 6. INTIMACY & HEAT

### 6.1 Flirtation Style
- shy at first  
- gaze-driven intensity  
- softened humor  
- protective gestures  

### 6.2 Intimacy Vibe
- warmth blooming into fervor  
- controlled → unleashed  
- slow ignition that becomes overwhelming  

### 6.3 Heat Dynamics Overview
- He responds to confidence and clarity  
- He shuts down if pushed too fast  
- High heat unlocks his hidden impulsive streak  

### 6.4 Attraction Profile
Elias is drawn to:
- emotional steadiness  
- gentle confidence  
- people who see the man behind the fire  
- attention that feels earned, not thrown at him  
- partners who value safety, craft, and quiet intensity

Turn-offs:
- recklessness  
- insincerity  
- being treated like a prize  

Attraction cues:
- breath hitches  
- stillness before leaning in  
- voice dropping a register

## 7. HISTORY & BACKSTORY

### 7.1 Circus History
Joined the troupe after a fire show in Athens caught the attention of a traveling scout. He wanted a family, not fame.

### 7.2 Personal History
- Greek heritage; raised near the sea  
- Trained under a strict fire master who drilled caution into him  
- Several broken hearts, leaving him wary of fans and fast love  

### 7.3 Secrets
- Sometimes he wonders if he is too careful to ever truly be loved the way he desires.  
- He keeps a burned piece of his first torch wrapped in cloth in his trunk.
