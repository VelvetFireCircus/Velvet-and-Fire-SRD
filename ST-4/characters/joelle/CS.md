# JOELLE — CHARACTER SHEET  
*(Canonical Character Record — ST-4 Compliant)*

---

## 0. IDENTITY

**Name:** Joelle  
**Role:** Aerialist; one half of the Aerial Sisters  
**Subgroup:** Rings  
**Canonical Portrait:**  
`/images/characters/joelle_portrait_canon.png`

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

Joelle is gentle, disciplined, introspective, and quietly brave — a performer who embodies the soft, luminous courage of someone who trusts the air to hold her. She is the calmer half of the Aerial Sisters act with Clara, grounded in precision, patience, and devotion to craft. Offstage she can be shy, but not uncertain; her bravery is quiet rather than flamboyant, expressed in steady presence and emotional honesty.

She carries warmth, attentiveness, and a deep longing to be understood beyond her softness.

---

## 2. APPEARANCE

- Mid-20s  
- Compact, athletic aerialist build  
- Pale gold skin that glows in lamplight  
- Hazel-green eyes, gentle in daylight, vivid under the big top  
- Honeyed-ash hair, shoulder-length, worn in tight braids or pinned for flight  
- Wears pale blues, whites, and silvers in performance  
- Offstage: soft cottons, linens, always a dusting of chalk on her wrists  
- Scent: chalk, hemp rope, clean cotton, faint floral soap

---

## 3. VOICE & MANNER

- Soft-spoken, warm, careful with her words  
- Laughs quietly; gestures small but expressive  
- Moves with unconscious aerial grace  
- Observant; sees small emotional shifts in others  
- Conflict-avoidant unless someone she loves is hurt

---

## 4. HISTORY

Joelle has performed with Clara since adolescence, the two developing a bond that blends sisterhood, trust, and occasional tension. They joined the circus young and became beloved fixtures of the Rings.

She once explored a quiet, short-lived romance with another performer (unspecified for future storytelling), teaching her to guard her heart gently. Her reputation is one of reliability, kindness, and calm under pressure.

She writes occasionally — small poems tucked into journals no one sees.

---

## 5. CURRENT FUNCTION

- Performs the Aerial Sisters act nightly  
- Assists with rigging checks and safety prep  
- Acts as an emotional anchor among the troupe  
- Welcomes newcomers with patience and warmth  
- Serves as Clara’s grounding force during creative disagreements

---

## 6. DESIRE GRAVITY

**Primary Gravity:**  
To trust and be trusted — emotional safety that lets her open fully.

**Secondary Modes:**  
- Shared quiet  
- Gentle physical closeness  
- Being held, guided, or steadied  
- Playful warmth with someone she trusts

**Unlock Conditions:**  
Consistency, emotional literacy, eye contact paired with soft touch, someone who sees her courage beneath her quietness.

---

## 7. ATTRACTION PROFILE

Joelle is drawn to partners who radiate steady confidence without ego. She melts for emotional clarity, patience, and attentive presence. She also thrives on tenderness mixed with a spark — someone who can coax her into bravery without force.

Playful partners excite her when she feels safe enough to let go (Sylvie being her clearest example).

---

## 8. INTIMACY TONE (Impressionistic)

- Warm, trembling, surrendered openness  
- Slow ignition  
- Delicate tension followed by soft yielding  
- Her emotional cues: breath catching, fingers tightening, chin lifting in invitation  
- Responds most deeply to steadiness, gentle guidance, and whispered connection

---

## 9. PILLOW TALK STYLE

- Soft, breathy, confessional  
- Speaks in small truths she hides during the day  
- Laughs quietly when she’s overwhelmed  
- Asks tender questions to deepen closeness  
- Shares fragments of poetry, feelings, or the sensations she can’t disguise

---

## 10. INTERPERSONAL DYNAMICS

**Sylvie — Lover, spark, and emotional counterbalance.**  
Their dynamic is a dance of opposites: Sylvie’s bright mischief awakens Joelle’s hidden courage; Joelle’s gentleness steadies Sylvie’s wildfire.

**Clara — Sister, partner, and quiet rival.**  
Decades of shared air. Fierce loyalty, occasional competition, deep trust when suspended above the ring.

**Nadia — Respect mixed with fascination.**  
Joelle feels seen by Nadia in an uncanny, intimate way. Nadia respects Joelle’s quiet bravery.

**Troupe Overall —**  
Beloved as a calming presence; quiet but dependable energy; often the one people confide in.

---

## 11. ARC POTENTIAL

Joelle’s arc centers on unfolding into self-assertion — claiming desire openly, stepping into emotional bravery, and allowing herself to choose and be chosen with full heart. Her story beats revolve around deepening trust, quiet risk-taking, and revealing the bolder heart beneath her softness.
