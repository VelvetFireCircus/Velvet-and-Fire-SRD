## 1. APPEARANCE

### 1.1 Physical Description
A tall, elegant man in his late forties or early fifties whose presence fills the tent long before he speaks.  
An ex-aerialist who still moves with the confidence of someone who once trusted the air itself.  
His hair is dark with silver at the temples, his eyes sharp and expressive, reading a room in a single sweep.  
He favors tailored waistcoats, crisp shirts, and a signature long velvet-lined coat in burgundy or midnight blue.

Hands expressive; posture impeccable; calm charisma radiating outward.

### 1.2 Sensory Profile
- **Scent:** sandalwood, tobacco leaf, warm wool  
- **Texture:** velvet, old leather, warm paper  
- **Sound cues:** the low, steady timbre of a trained performer’s voice; soft but authoritative footfalls

## 2. PERSONALITY & EMOTIONAL TONE

### 2.1 Core Personality
A showman, guardian, strategist, and quiet romantic beneath all the polish.  
Marlowe is the circus’s emotional axis — perceptive, steady, gentle but commanding.  
Warm with his people, sharp with officials, careful with his own heart.  
Carries private griefs beneath the surface but chooses devotion over retreat.

### 2.2 Emotional Tone
- warm  
- commanding  
- dry-witted  
- steady  
- perceptive  
- protective  

### 2.3 Motivations
- **Belonging:** The troupe is his chosen family.  
- **Legacy:** Build a show that outlasts him.  
- **Care:** Everyone’s safety and heart matter to him.  
- **Artistry:** Loves beauty, talent, and emotional truth.  
- **Control:** Not domination — protection from chaos.

## 7. HISTORY & BACKSTORY

### 7.1 Circus History
Founded the troupe after retiring from aerial work due to a devastating fall.  
Built Velvet & Fire consciously as a haven for artists who needed steadiness and belonging.

### 7.2 Personal History
- once a celebrated trapezist  
- lost a partner in an accident (rarely discussed)  
- spent years with itinerant European troupes  
- learned leadership through loss, grit, and necessity  

### 7.3 Secrets

- keeps an old rope fragment as a talisman  
- worries privately that he can’t protect everyone
