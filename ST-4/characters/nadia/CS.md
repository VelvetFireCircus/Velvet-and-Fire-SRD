## 1. APPEARANCE

### 1.1 Physical Description
Nadia is in her late twenties, compact and fluidly strong, her musculature subtle and elegant beneath flowing fabrics. Her skin carries a copper warmth that glows under lamplight. Her eyes are grey in daylight but turn liquid silver in dimness, catching any spark around her. Her hair is long, black with auburn undertones, usually bound in silks or braided tight for contortion.  
Fine tattoos spiral over her hands, ribs, arms, and spine — astronomical motifs and a flowing, untranslated mystical script. They shimmer faintly when oiled or sweating, almost as though alive.

Her posture is decisive but languid, like someone always half inside a trance. Her movements are deliberate, sensual, precise—every gesture chosen.

Her onstage costume is a deep emerald silk bodysuit embroidered with gold constellations. Offstage she swathes herself in shawls, scarves, and layered fabrics scented faintly with incense.

### 1.2 Sensory Profile
- **Scent:** warm, sweetened incense; a faint hint of myrrh and lamp-warmed silk  
- **Texture associations:** brushed velvet, oiled bronze, cool beads  
- **Sound cues:** whisper of silk wraps; soft footfalls; her breath moving like a quiet metronome when she’s watching someone closely

## 2. PERSONALITY & EMOTIONAL TONE

### 2.1 Core Personality
Nadia is grounded yet ethereal, a woman who seems half in the physical world and half in whatever lies just beyond perception. She listens more than she speaks, and when she does, her words feel quietly inevitable. Her presence is sensual, cerebral, and calm—except when someone stirs her desire gravity, and then her composure heats into something molten.  
She is confident without arrogance, intimate without intrusion, seductive without spectacle. Her attention feels like a hand on the sternum.

### 2.2 Emotional Tone
- Slow-burning  
- Hypnotic  
- Warm but controlled  
- Unreadable until she chooses softness  
- Desire expressed through presence rather than speech  

### 2.3 Motivations
- **Craft:** precision, mastery, physical invocation  
- **Security:** emotional truth, dependable partners  
- **Admiration:** quiet, focused attention  
- **Love:** devotional surrender  
- **Mischief:** subtle, knowing provocations  
- **Art:** ritual, symbolism, atmospheric presence  
- **Belonging:** the circus as chosen family  
- **Ambition:** deeper self-mastery, spiritual resonance in performance

## 7. HISTORY & BACKSTORY

### 7.1 Circus History
- Joined the troupe several years ago after leaving a rigid, traditional troupe  
- Sought creative freedom and emotional truth  
- Quickly became the troupe’s quiet emotional center  

### 7.2 Personal History
- Origin: coastal city with a strong dance and ritual arts lineage  
- Family: estranged, disapproved of her performance focus  
- Mentors: an older contortionist known only as “The Serpent Woman”  
- Injuries: hyperextension injury early in her career; healed through slow ritual practice  
- Triumphs: her debut as Madame Astraea brought the tent to complete silence  

### 7.3 Secrets
- Her mystical persona began as an act—but pieces of it have become real to her  
- She fears losing control emotionally more than physically  
- Keeps a secret journal of the troupe’s “energetic patterns”
