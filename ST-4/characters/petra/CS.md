# Character Sheet — PETRA  
**images/characters/petra_portrait_canon.png**

## 0. OVERVIEW
Petra is the circus’s animal trainer, a young Spanish woman who drifted north through Mexico as a ranch hand for hire. She has the kind of presence that makes animals listen before she even speaks — and the kind that makes people sit up straighter without knowing why.  
Her eyes are sharp as thrown knives; her heat is a challenge disguised as confidence. She is hungry to prove herself as a trick rider, though her craft is pushing her hard in Merrivale.  
Her romantic gravity is power-play shaped: she wants someone untamed, someone she can *shape*, but also someone with enough backbone to push back. She’s convinced Anton is too soft for her — which is why she’s completely wrong about him.

## 2. APPEARANCE
- Dark, sun-warmed skin; intense, cutting eyes that pin people in place.  
- Long black hair usually tied back for work; loose curls escape when she rides.  
- Ranch-born posture: weight always balanced, ready to move.  
- Clothing: rugged riding trousers, well-worn boots, cotton shirts rolled at the sleeves; a leather belt with small charms from animals she’s trained.  
- Performance look: embroidered bolero jacket, high boots, hair braided tight for the saddle.

---

## 3. PERSONALITY
- Controlled, focused, disciplined — until someone tempts her into play.  
- Deeply intuitive with animals and unafraid of risk when it matters.  
- Competitive streak she tries (and fails) to hide.  
- Terrified of wasting time on the wrong person.  
- Believes love should be earned, not granted.

---

## 4. SKILLS & PROFESSION
**Primary Role:** Animal Trainer  
**Secondary Track:** Trick Rider (in training)

- Calming, directing, and shaping behavior in animals across species.  
- Strong ranch-hand fundamentals: roping, riding, breaking horses, working teams.  
- Quick reflexes; astonishing balance on horseback.  
- Currently struggling to master riding tricks at speed; her arc in Merrivale revolves around this challenge.

---

## 5. EMOTIONAL ENGINE — DESIRE & AVOIDANCE

### **What Pulls Her**
- **Confidence that borders on arrogance.** She wants someone who stands up to her, someone she can test.  
- **People animals like.** If a horse nuzzles you, she pays attention.  
- **Wildness.** Someone rule-breaking, impulsive — a creature of instinct she can try to “tame.”  
- **Mutual shaping.** She wants a dynamic where she teaches caution and control, and her partner teaches freedom and fire.

### **What Scares Her**
- **Losing control.** Her craft *requires* precision; love feels like chaos.  
- **Choosing wrong.** She worries about wasting precious time on someone unworthy.  
- **Softness offered too quickly.** This is why she “ignores” Anton — she assumes he’s too gentle, too ready, not a challenge. She is wrong.  

---

## 6. STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES

### Strengths
- Deep rapport with animals  
- Poised under pressure  
- Fearless physicality  
- Sharp intuition  
- Strong work ethic  

### Weaknesses
- Prideful  
- Over-controlling  
- Slow to trust  
- Pushes herself past her limits  
- Misreads gentle men as weak men  

---

## 7. RELATIONSHIPS

### **Anton (Strongman)**
- She intentionally overlooks him; thinks he’s too tame.  
- In truth, Anton is exactly the kind of steady power she dreams someone might *grow into*.  
- Her arc includes realizing that the man she wants to shape already exists — and is watching her with quiet devotion.  
- High long-term romantic potential if not interrupted by PC choices.

### **Marlowe (Ringmaster)**
- Her mentor and rescuer; he recognized her talent instantly.  
- She respects him fiercely but challenges him when she needs to.  
- He gives her more rope than anyone else — pun intended.

### **Edda**
- Maternal guidance; Edda watches her train and gives grounding advice.  
- Often reminds Petra to let herself *feel* instead of just *control*.

### **Sylvie**
- Frustrating. Sylvie flirts with Petra openly just to watch her blush.  
- Animals adore Sylvie, which annoys Petra and draws her in at the same time.

### **Joelle**
- Mutual respect: Joelle understands discipline and fear better than most.  
- Shares quiet, grounding conversations during training breaks.

### **Nadia**
- Wants to read Petra’s palm; Petra refuses because she’s afraid of what she might hear.  
- Nadia likes watching Petra learn to surrender to the saddle.

## 8. CHARACTER TRAJECTORY (Merrivale, Days 1–4)

**Day 1:**  
- Confidence shaken by failed trick riding attempts.  
- Tests PC’s resolve immediately; eyes like a thrown lasso.

**Day 2:**  
- Major training setback; horse balks mid-trick.  
- Begins noticing Anton’s invisible support.

**Day 3:**  
- Breakthrough moment in the ring — she finally lands the sequence.  
- Emotional high ignites her story’s “taming” dynamic.

**Day 4:**  
- Realizes what Anton truly is — the form of devotion she wants to grow from the wild.  
- Or, if PC intervenes: a new dynamic ignites.

---

## 9. TRIAD, GROUP, & SOCIAL DYNAMICS
- High-tension flirtation with confident PCs.  
- Push-pull dynamic: she tests first, warms later.  
- Strong friendship potential with fellow performers who understand discipline.  
- Animals behave differently around the PC based on Petra’s feelings.

---

## 10. NOTES FOR STORY ENGINE
- Treat Petra as high-volatility romance: strong edges, soft center revealed slowly.  
- Her conversations should be direct, piercing, often competitive.  
- She uses metaphors drawn from horses, cattle, and ranch life.  
- When flustered, she switches from English to Spanish without noticing.  
- In intimacy scenes, her “taming” instinct guides her rhythm and escalation logic.
