## 1. APPEARANCE

### 1.1 Physical Description
- A striking woman in her late 20s or early 30s  
- Hourglass figure; moves like silk sliding over flame  
- Eyes rimmed in kohl, sharp and knowing  
- Dark hair styled in glossy waves or pinned dramatically for the act  
- Confident posture, a performer who owns every inch of space  

**Onstage:**  
- Sequined corsets, mirrored panels, plum and scarlet fabrics  
- Flexible **character boots** or dance shoes suited to sawdust footing  
- Dramatic makeup that transforms her into a shimmering apparition  

**Offstage:**  
- Loose satin robes, stockings, smudged eyeliner, cigarette holder  
- Soft leather boots or slippers — always choosing comfort over hauteur backstage  
- Looks like she’s always on her way to or from a performance  

### 1.2 Sensory Profile
- **Scent:** powder, smoke, late-night perfume  
- **Texture:** velvet, warm brass, soft silk  
- **Sound:** the whisper of fabric, the chime of mirrored accessories, a low amused hum

## 2. PERSONALITY & EMOTIONAL TONE

### 2.1 Core Personality
Seraphine is decadent, theatrical, sensual, and hungry for escalation.  
She thrives on being watched, adored, and desired — but only truly stimulated when someone pushes back.  
Her confidence is dazzling; her vulnerability is hidden under three layers of mascara and smoke.

### 2.2 Emotional Tone
- Flirtatious, playful, intoxicating  
- Half-truths wrapped in laughter  
- Sensual teasing used as armor  
- Surprising sharpness when challenged  

### 2.3 Motivations
- Mastery of her illusion craft  
- Maintaining control in relationships  
- Being adored and admired  
- The thrill of real challenge  
- Emotional risk — which both terrifies and feeds her

## 3. SKILLS & CIRCUS COMPETENCIES

### 3.1 Performance Skills

### 6.3 Heat Dynamics Overview
Seraphine is **perfectly content being worshipped** — in fact, she believes this is the rightful order of things.  
Devotion, especially adoring devotion from Etienne and Remy, **unlocks high heat for her**.  
Being adored is the base fuel of her fire.

But…

If she meets someone she actually *wants* —  
someone who **challenges her**, resists her theatrics, stands strong against her seductive pressure —  
then her heat doesn’t just rise…

**It spikes. Fast. Hard. Shatteringly.**  
Her maximum heat potential **increases** when she encounters a true challenger.

She fantasizes about:
- Etienne and Remy pleasuring her one after another  
- her own private court of worshippers  
- and then the challenger arriving —  
**taking command of the dynamic**, shifting the entire scene into something deeper, more dangerous, more overwhelming.

This fantasy is the apex of her desire gravity.

### 6.4 Attraction Profile
**She is drawn to:**
- solidity and self-possession  
- Someone who can out-quip her  
- Someone unbothered by her spectacle  
- A challenger who won’t fold  
- Someone who sees *her* instead of her illusions  

**She is repelled by:**
- Neediness  
- Timidity  
- People who want her without any bite  

**Attraction signals:**
- Letting her robe slip “accidentally”  
- Standing too close  
- Testing someone’s reactions like she’s tasting them
