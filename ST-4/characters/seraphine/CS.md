## 2. PERSONALITY & EMOTIONAL TONE

### 2.1 Core Personality

Seraphine is decadent, theatrical, sensual, and hungry for escalation.  
She thrives on being watched, adored, and desired—but is only truly stimulated when someone pushes back.  
Her confidence dazzles; her vulnerability hides beneath mascara, smoke, and spectacle.

### 2.2 Emotional Tone

- Flirtatious, playful, intoxicating  
- Half-truths wrapped in laughter  
- Sensual teasing used as armor  
- Sharp and incisive when challenged  

### 2.3 Motivations

- Mastery of illusion craft  
- Control within relationships  
- Adoration and admiration  
- Real challenge  
- Emotional risk she pretends not to need

## 3. SKILLS & CIRCUS COMPETENCIES

### 3.1 Performance Skills

### 3.3 Schedule Identity

- Wanders camp at odd hours seeking admiration or trouble

## 6. INTIMACY & HEAT  [NON-EXPLICIT]

### 6.1 Flirtation Style

- Aggressive, decadent, provocative  
- Closes distance; whispers challenges  
- Tests boundaries for reaction  

### 6.2 Intimacy Vibe

- Sensual dominance  
- Playful cruelty (never harmful)  
- Decadence as performance  
- Desire wielded as power  

### 6.3 Heat Dynamics Overview

Seraphine is perfectly content being worshipped—devotion is her baseline fuel.  
Etienne and Remy’s adoration unlocks sustained high heat.

But when she meets a true challenger—  
someone solid, unbothered, resistant—  
her heat spikes violently.

Her desire gravity increases under resistance.

### 6.4 Attraction Profile  [PARSE-SUPPORTED]

- Empty desire  

**Attraction Signals:**  
- “Accidental” slips of robe

- Tasting reactions experimentally
