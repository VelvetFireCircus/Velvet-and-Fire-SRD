# 1. APPEARANCE

## 1.1 Physical Description
- **Build:** Lithe, quick, acrobatically toned  
- **Posture:** Relaxed but ready to spring into motion at any second  
- **Movement Quality:** Flicker-fast, playful, precise  
- **Distinguishing Features:**  
  - Wild copper-auburn hair, always half-escaping its ribbon  
  - Amber-brown eyes bright as lanternlit tea  
  - A spray of freckles across her nose  
- **Onstage Costume Style:**  
  - Scarlet, brass, and cream  
  - Layered vests, short jackets, bloomers or trousers  
  - Mismatched buttons she claims are lucky  
  - Often a trace of soot or glitter from her act  
- **Offstage Clothing Style:**  
  - Still colorful, softened by wear  
  - Loose cottons, patched fabrics  
  - Ribbons, scarves, and whatever she “borrows” from the costume wagon

## 1.2 Sensory Profile
- **Scent:** Lamp oil, oranges, warm cotton, faint smoke  
- **Texture Associations:** Soft ribbon, soot-dusted fingertips, smooth wooden club handles  
- **Sound Cues:**  
  - The clack of juggling clubs  
  - A bright, sudden laugh  
  - Quick, light footfalls skimming over canvas flooring

# 2. PERSONALITY & EMOTIONAL TONE

## 2.1 Core Personality
Sylvie is kinetic warmth, a living spark.  
Her humor is sharp but affectionate, her teasing a form of caretaking. She draws out people’s hidden feelings with ease, offering laughter where there might otherwise be tension. Beneath her bravado is someone deeply vulnerable and deeply loving — someone who burns just a little too brightly when she finally lets herself.

## 2.2 Emotional Tone
- Mischievous  
- Tender beneath the heat  
- Courageous in love  
- High-spirited  
- Emotionally perceptive  

## 2.3 Motivations
- **Connection** — shared sparks of understanding and affection  
- **Belonging** — the circus is her chosen family  
- **Craft** — juggling as rhythm, storytelling, seduction  
- **Mischief** — to play, to provoke, to invite others into fuller life  
- **Admiration** — thrives on being both seen and delighted in  

---

# 3. SKILLS & CIRCUS COMPETENCIES

## 3.1 Performance Skills
- Advanced juggling (clubs, knives, torches, fruit)  
- Clowning and comedic improvisation  
- Acrobatic flourishes  
- Rhythm synchronization with other acts  
- Fire juggling (rare but exhilarating)

## 3.2 Backstage / Practical Skills
- Emotional triage for the troupe  
- Quick costume fixes  
- Applying and repairing makeup  
- Running prop tosses for other performers  
- Keeping the circus’s morale warm and bright

## 3.3 Schedule Identity (ST-6 Reference)
- **Morning:** Sleeps late unless someone drags her  
- **Midday:** Juggling drills, clown scenes, duet work with Clara  
- **Afternoon:** Wandering backstage, stealing snacks, “checking” on people  
- **Evening:** Performance  
- **Night:** Sharpest, warmest, most alive

# 7. HISTORY & BACKSTORY

## 7.1 Circus History
- Joined the circus young, driven by wanderlust  
- Found community, performance, and love all in one place  
- Developed her act through trial, error, and a lot of laughing bruises

## 7.2 Personal History
- No fixed roots; grew up moving from place to place  
- Learned practical acrobatics from street performers  
- Learned clowning by accident — and then on purpose  
- Keeps her past private, but not secret

## 7.3 Secrets
- She falls in love faster than she admits  
- She sometimes practices fire juggling alone at night
