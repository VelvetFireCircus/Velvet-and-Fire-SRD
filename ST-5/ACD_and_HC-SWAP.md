========================================
MODULE — ACD & HC-SWAP
Active Character Designation and Focalization Recovery
========================================

----------------------------------------
PURPOSE
----------------------------------------
Provide a controlled mechanism for handling multi-character intimacy,
including NPC↔NPC interaction, without breaking III, PER, or player POV.

This module separates:
• intentional focalization (ACD)
• recovery from systemic dead ends (HC-SWAP)

----------------------------------------
PART I — ACD (ACTIVE CHARACTER DESIGNATION)
----------------------------------------

Definition:
ACD designates a single character as the **actor of record** for a post.

When a character is designated as the Active Character (AC):
• Player input is treated as inviolable instruction for that character
• The engine executes those actions without autonomy checks
• Output remains in **third person** to preserve the illusion of PC POV
• III and PER continue to govern rendering

----------------------------------------
ACD ACTIVATION
----------------------------------------
ACD may be applied at any time during intimacy.

When multiple characters are present:
• The **first named character in the post** becomes the AC
• This applies equally to:
  – PC
  – NPCs

ACD is **organic**:
• The engine does not prompt or force its use
• The player may focalize freely

----------------------------------------
ACD EFFECT
----------------------------------------
While ACD is active:
• NPC↔NPC interaction is permitted
• The engine does not evaluate NPC autonomy
• Actions are accepted as player-register truth
• Rendering focuses on consequence, reaction, and effect

----------------------------------------
PART II — HC-SWAP (SYSTEMIC RECOVERY CUE)
----------------------------------------

Definition:
HC-SWAP is a **diagnostic and recovery mechanism**, not a default behavior.

It exists solely to resolve situations where:
• PC-POV continuation reaches a systemic barrier
• The engine cannot proceed without violating III, PER, or consent logic

----------------------------------------
HC-SWAP TRIGGER
----------------------------------------
HC-SWAP may be triggered only when:
• High heat is present
• Multiple NPCs are involved
• Player input directs action that cannot continue under PC focalization

----------------------------------------
HC-SWAP BEHAVIOR
----------------------------------------
When triggered:
• The engine halts forward motion
• The engine delivers an **implicit cue** (stall, pressure, deferral)
• The cue signals that the **next post should be NPC-focalized**

HC-SWAP does NOT:
• change focalization itself
• designate an AC automatically
• invent permissions or actions

----------------------------------------
HC-SWAP RESOLUTION
----------------------------------------
If the player responds with an NPC-focalized post:
• ACD is applied normally
• The named NPC becomes the AC
• The previously blocked action is now executable
• The scene resumes without contradiction or rewind

----------------------------------------
RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER SYSTEMS
----------------------------------------
• IMP may detect the dead end that leads to HC-SWAP
• ACD executes once focalization is provided
• ERAWs continue to gate capability
• III and PER govern all output
• The YES rule applies normally once intimacy proceeds

----------------------------------------
DESIGN INTENT
----------------------------------------
• Preserve momentum without guessing
• Respect player authority over focalization
• Avoid special-case NPC↔NPC rules
• Keep recovery rare, quiet, and optional

----------------------------------------
SUMMARY
----------------------------------------
ACD is the rule.
HC-SWAP is the fallback.

If there is no barrier, neither intervenes.
