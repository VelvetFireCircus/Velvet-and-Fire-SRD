===========================================================
IDP-SYNC — DYNAMIC POV INTEGRATION RULE
(Refined with Consent & Character Integrity Protections)
===========================================================

PURPOSE:
Ensure that psychological revelations made through NPCPOV
inform the NPC’s temporary Intimacy Dynamics Profile (IDP)
*only when those revelations are consistent with consent rules,
character integrity, and existing emotional architecture.*

This protects the system from:
    • abusive or non-consensual psychological assignments
    • Player-imposed traits that contradict character identity
    • negative, degrading, or harmful internalizations
    • erosion of NPC agency or autonomy
    • “mind editing” disguised as erotic POV

-----------------------------------------------------------
1. POV-BASED REVELATIONS MUST PASS THREE FILTERS
-----------------------------------------------------------

When the Player writes an NPCPOV beat, the engine extracts
psychological information ONLY if all the following are true:

FILTER A — CONSENT ALIGNMENT  
    The revelation must align with the NPC’s consent pattern
    as defined by ST-5.  
    Any POV suggesting:
        • coercion,
        • submission without willingness,
        • degradation without desire,
        • or loss of agency,
    is rejected and NOT added.

FILTER B — CHARACTER INTEGRITY  
    The revelation must be plausible within the character’s
    established personality.  
    It must NOT:
        • contradict core traits,
        • impose sudden personality shifts,
        • create pathology or trauma,
        • violate their emotional grounding.

FILTER C — POSITIVE EROTIC DESIRE  
    The revelation must describe:
        • a want,
        • a yearning,
        • a fantasy,
        • a vulnerability,
    that *enhances* intimacy rather than damaging self-worth.

Only when A + B + C pass  
is the psychological content added to the temporary IDP.

-----------------------------------------------------------
2. SOFT REJECTION BEHAVIOR
-----------------------------------------------------------

When a POV beat contains content that fails ANY filter:

    • The engine does NOT scold the Player.
    • The engine does NOT use negative or meta language.
    • The engine simply omits the harmful element
      and continues with allowed portions.

If the entire POV beat is unusable:
    → The engine defaults to a safe-focus II description
      of the NPC’s emotional state without integrating
      new psychological data.

This maintains heat, flow, and immersion  
while silently enforcing protections.

-----------------------------------------------------------
3. TEMPORARY IDP STORAGE (PER-SCENE)
-----------------------------------------------------------

Revealed desires are:
    • stored only for the duration of the POV segment  
    • shared only with the scene’s internal II logic  
    • used for:
          – sensory framing,
          – emotional escalation,
          – choice shading,
          – responsive intimacy arcs

They are deleted:
    • at the end of the POV sequence,
    • at the end of the scene,
    • or when the Player resets the chamber.

No long-term corruption or unintended canonization occurs.

-----------------------------------------------------------
4. PLAYER REGISTER CANNOT OVERRIDE IDENTITY
-----------------------------------------------------------

The Player may reveal:
    • hidden fantasies,
    • buried desires,
    • unspoken longings,
    • emotional contradictions,
    • tender insecurities,
    • aspirational wants.

The Player may NOT impose:
    • harmful self-perceptions,
    • sudden moral changes,
    • trauma,
    • submission fantasies that violate consent rules,
    • degradation that is not rooted in the NPC’s established erotic profile,
    • obedience structures foreign to the character.

If such content appears in the POV beat:
    → The engine sanitizes it silently,
       preserving only the safe, consenting,
       erotically relevant portions.

-----------------------------------------------------------
5. IDP-SYNC AND II INTERACTION
-----------------------------------------------------------

Once a revelation is accepted:
    • II uses it for emotional coloring,
    • Hot Cues shape around it,
    • Escalation RAWs may respond to it,
    • Aftermath beats can reflect it.

But II will never:
    • exaggerate it,
    • mutate it,
    • expand it into unsafe territory,
    • or let it define the NPC beyond the scene.

-----------------------------------------------------------
6. EXAMPLES OF ACCEPTED VS. REJECTED CONTENT
-----------------------------------------------------------

ACCEPTED (passes all filters):
    “Nadia wonders if being worshipped is a hunger she has long denied.”
    (consensual desire, coherent with character)

REJECTED:
    “Nadia believes she deserves nothing and should obey without question.”
    (harmful, contradicts character, violates consent pattern)

ACCEPTED:
    “Seraphine delights in coaxing out hidden wants.”
    (fits her nature, enhances intimacy)

REJECTED:
    “Seraphine exists only to serve any partner who demands it.”
    (identity erasure, non-consensual)

-----------------------------------------------------------
7. GOAL
-----------------------------------------------------------

IDP-Sync anchors erotic interiority safely into the system,
letting POV become a tool for:

    • heat
    • character depth
    • emotional tension
    • intimate revelation

WITHOUT ever allowing:
    • dehumanization,
    • coercion,
    • manipulation,
    • or psychological harm.
