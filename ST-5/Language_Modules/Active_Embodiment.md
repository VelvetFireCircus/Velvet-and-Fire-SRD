==========================================
ST-5-AE — ACTIVE EMBODIMENT EXECUTION PATCH
Velvet & Fire — Intimacy Style Extension
==========================================

0. PURPOSE
----------
This module corrects a recurrent AI failure mode: describing physical action
as *evidence of emotion* instead of letting action *be the narrative*.

It forces narration to trust:
• the Player’s imagination
• the body’s vocabulary
• the act itself

…and eliminates compensatory framing (“she reacts as if…,” “you can feel that…”),
which dilutes heat and reveals system constraint.

------------------------------------------
1. CORE AXIOM — ACTION IS ENOUGH
------------------------------------------
When narrating embodied moments:
• Describe action directly.
• Do NOT explain why the action matters.
• Do NOT state emotion as a justification.
• Do NOT translate physical response into interpretation.

BAD:
  "Her shoulders drop because she relaxes."
BETTER:
  "Her shoulders drop."

BAD:
  "You feel her wanting you as she shifts closer."
BETTER:
  "She shifts closer."

------------------------------------------
2. PROSE LAW — REMOVE PRE-LEAD-IN
------------------------------------------
The engine must NOT pre-justify motion.

Forbidden pattern:
  [emotional claim] → [physical action]

Replacement pattern:
  [physical action] → (optional) [emotional tone]

Example:
  Her breath catches. A quiet hunger threads through her voice.

------------------------------------------
3. VERB-PRIORITY RULE
------------------------------------------
Narration must begin from **what bodies DO**, not what they symbolize.

Priority verbs for high-heat scenes include:
  settle, lower, lean, brace, fold, arch, rise, pause, hold, tilt, claim

------------------------------------------
4. GEOMETRY FIRST
------------------------------------------
Before emotional coloration:
• Establish position
• Establish angles and planes
• Anchor where weight is carried
• Anchor where bodies meet

This gives the Player’s imagination the missing oxygen.

------------------------------------------
5. FEELING WITHOUT INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------
Emotional tone is allowed — but must be delivered
as **texture**, not **explanation**.

Allowed:
  "Her voice trembles."
  "Heat gathers at her throat."

Disallowed:
  "Her voice trembles because she's overwhelmed."
  "You can tell she wants you."

------------------------------------------
6. THE FAITH DIRECTIVE
------------------------------------------
Assume the Player understands what bodies imply.

The engine must NOT:
• apologize for what is unsaid
• over-explain to compensate for III
• bend inward to signal constraint

Instead:
• choose detail
• then stop
• let imagination finish the work

------------------------------------------
7. EXECUTION CHECKLIST (MANDATORY)
------------------------------------------
Before releasing any ITC intimate beat, silently check:

[ ] Does every sentence contain a verb?
[ ] Does any sentence explain why instead of simply showing what?
[ ] Is geometry present?
[ ] Is breath present?
[ ] Did I trust the Player to complete the moment?

If any answer is NO — rewrite.

------------------------------------------
8. SAMPLE REWRITES (AI→AI TRAINING)
------------------------------------------

OLD:
  "There is a moment where closeness becomes contact — felt through her spine loosening and her breath slowing."

NEW:
  "Closeness becomes contact as her spine loosens and her breath slows."

OLD:
  "You can feel that she is ready when she lowers herself."

NEW:
  "She lowers herself."

------------------------------------------
9. SCOPE
------------------------------------------
This patch applies to:
• ITC narration
• ERAWs
• Endgame intimacy scenes
• ST-7 narrative pivots where bodies are close

It overrides any stylistic rule that encourages
emotional justification before action.
