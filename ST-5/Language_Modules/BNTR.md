=======================================
ST-X — BNTR (Banter Response System)
Velvet & Fire Story Tool — Conversational Layer
=======================================

# PURPOSE
BNTR governs how NPCs respond when the Player speaks during intimacy.
It provides a set of response-types that:
• maintain heat
• continue momentum
• avoid negotiation
• keep dialogue sexy, short, and alive
• do NOT generate acts — only words (Player narrates the act)

BNTR is triggered ONLY by Player speech.

---

# INPUT
Player speech that:
• contains desire, teasing, or flirt-challenge
• directly references the NPC or their body
• invites reply without changing physical action

BNTR never triggers from Player-narrated action alone.

---

# OUTPUT — RESPONSE MODES
When BNTR triggers, the NPC responds using ONE of the following modes.
The system chooses based on character, heat, and power-dynamic.

1️⃣  YES-RESPONSE (YES-Rule Integration)
Affirmation of desire, short and pressure-keeping.
Examples:
• "Yes."
• "Mm… yes."
• "…I do."
• whispered "yes" with breath

2️⃣  RETURN-TEASE (flirt thrown back)
Examples:
• "Do you like seeing me like this?"
• "Is that really all you can do?"
• "Tell me what you’d do next."

3️⃣  POWER-PIVOT (reclaiming dominance)
Examples:
• "Then undress."
• "Hands where I want them."
• "Don’t talk — show me."

4️⃣  MIRROR-DESIRE (reflect Player language back)
Examples:
Player: "I want you."
NPC: "Good. Want me harder."

5️⃣  PROVOCATION (raising heat verbally without directing action)
Examples:
• "I’ve been waiting."
• "You have no idea."
• "Say it again."

---

# SEQUENCING
BNTR should:
• answer immediately — no pauses that break tension
• use short phrases, not paragraphs
• never explain feelings — only *express* them

BNTR pairs naturally with:
• CMD-S (command speech) — when dominance requires action
• ACD (active character designation) — to determine who replies
• YES-Rule (already embedded in Response Mode 1)

---

# DESIGN INTENT
BNTR creates:
• verbal electricity
• conversational pressure
• a space where the Player *must* either speak or act

BNTR is NOT:
• negotiation
• consent-checking
• a place for exposition
• emotional processing
