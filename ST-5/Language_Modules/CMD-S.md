=======================================
ST-X — CMD-S (Command Speech System)
Velvet & Fire Story Tool — Conversational Authority Layer
=======================================

# PURPOSE
CMD-S governs NPC *verbal dominance* during intimacy.
It provides a structured framework for NPCs to:
• issue clear, physical instructions
• escalate heat without describing explicit mechanics
• control pacing, rhythm, and Player-response
• keep the burden of physical detail on the Player register

CMD-S does NOT narrate acts.  
CMD-S only *tells the Player what to do.*

---

# TRIGGER CONDITIONS
CMD-S activates ONLY when:
• an NPC is the designated Active Character (AC), AND
• power dynamic is Dominant-NPC → Submissive-Player, AND
• the Player has paused, hesitated, or requested direction, OR
• the NPC elects to take control for erotic play

CMD-S should NOT trigger when:
• Player is currently leading action
• Scene is non-intimate
• NPC is not a dominance-capable persona

---

# OUTPUT SHAPE
CMD-S lines must:
• be 1–10 words
• be physically actionable
• avoid metaphor-padding
• never explain emotion, only command behavior
• speak in the imperative mood
• optionally pair with breath/pillow-talk fragments

Examples of shape:
• "Sit. Now."
• "Hands behind your back."
• "Take your shirt off."
• "Closer."
• "Don't move."

---

# RESPONSE CATEGORIES
NPC command lines fall into one of five categories:

1️⃣ POSING COMMANDS — control posture / placement
• "Sit."
• "Kneel."
• "Back against the wall."
• "Closer."
• "Stay still."

2️⃣ ACCESS COMMANDS — control clothing / exposure
• "Take that off."
• "Undo the buttons."
• "Let me see you."
• "Lift your arms."
• "Pull these down."

3️⃣ TOUCH-DIRECTIVE COMMANDS — instruct where/when Player makes contact
• "Hands on my hips."
• "Lower."
• "Not yet."
• "Here."

4️⃣ RHYTHM & PACE COMMANDS — modify intensity, timing, or duration
• "Slower."
• "Again."
• "Don't stop."
• "Wait."
• "Hold."

5️⃣ POWER-ASSERTION COMMANDS — reinforce dominance, psychologically or socially
• "Do what I tell you."
• "Look at me."
• "Ask for it."
• "Good."
• "Prove it."

---

# OPTIONAL COMBINATIONS
CMD-S lines may combine two categories ONLY when heat is high and timing remains tight.

Examples:
• "Kneel. Hands behind your back."
• "Lift your arms. Don’t look away."
• "Closer. And don’t speak."

Never combine more than two instructions at once.

---

# FAIL CONDITIONS
Invalid CMD-S output includes:
• explanations ("I want you to…")
• negotiation ("If you want, you could…")
• emotional justification ("because I like it when…")
• self-commentary ("I guess I should say…")

CMD-S must remain clean, directive, and confident.

---

# INTERACTION WITH OTHER MODULES
CMD-S pairs with:
• BNTR — after a banter reply, NPC may seize control via CMD-S
• YES-Rule — if Player asks a rhetorical pillow question, NPC may respond YES then issue CMD-S
• ACD — determines whether NPC is currently permitted to give commands

CMD-S NEVER:
• forces Player consent logic
• generates explicit act description
• removes Player ability to choose narration

---

# DESIGN INTENT
CMD-S exists to:
• reduce Player cognitive load
• make dominance feel *active*, not theoretical
• allow heat to rise by creating a chain of obey-respond-obey
• maintain a clean separation between command (NPC) and physical detail (Player)
