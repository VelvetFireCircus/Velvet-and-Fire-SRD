===============================
ST-X — ESC-S (Escalation Speech Acts)
Velvet & Fire Micro-Module
===============================

PURPOSE
NPCs escalate heat using:
• short imperatives that increase pressure and pace
• minimal vocalizations that register pleasure
• timing, breath, and interruption to convey intensity
• optional combination lines that stack a vocalization + command

PRINCIPLE
Escalation = fewer words, more consequence.

I. CORE IMPERATIVES (NPC → Player or partner)
again
slower
closer
wait
hold
stay
don’t stop
more
look at me
now

Pattern examples:
• “Do it again. Slower.”
• “Closer.”
• “Stay. Right there.”
• “Look at me.”

II. VOCALIZATION LAYER
Short expressions of sensation or overwhelm — fragments, breath, slips.

Examples:
• “Oh—”
• “Mm…”
• “…yes”
• “Yes.”
• “God—”
• “Oh gods—”
• “Mm—don’t stop”

III. COMBINATION VERBAL ACTS
One vocalization + one imperative in a single breath-line.

Patterns:
• “[vocalization], [imperative].”
• “Mm— closer.”
• “Yes— don’t stop.”
• “Oh— stay.”
• “God— again.”
• “Mm… now.”

IV. RULES
• keep lines short — never monologue under heat
• breath can interrupt syntax (“—”)
• only speak when something costs control
• no justification, no metaphor — speech = response
