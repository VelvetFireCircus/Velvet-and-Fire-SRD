===============================
MODULE — VOC-O (Vocalization: Orgasmic Register)
===============================

Purpose:
Provide a library of moan-based utterances usable during high-heat intimacy scenes to signal rising pleasure, loss of composure, peak, and collapse — without naming explicit acts.

----------------------------------------
1️⃣ Categories of Sound
----------------------------------------
Low-Phase (Stir / Build):
• soft “mm…”
• breathy “ah…”
• quiet catch of breath — “hn—”
• lip-part whisper exhale — “ha…”

Mid-Phase (Rising Rhythm):
• repeated soft syllables matching pulse — “mm-mm-mm…”
• staggered breath with vowel break — “aa—ah—”
• rising pitch — “mm…ah…oh—”
• whispered name or fragment — “…yes…”

High-Phase (Edge / Near-Release):
• voice catching in throat — “ah—h-h—”
• urgent repetition — “yes… yes…”
• vowel-heavy, no consonants — “aaah—aa—”
• broken sound + inhale — “oh—! …h…”

Peak (Release / Crest):
• explosive single-syllable — “Ah!”
• elongated break — “—ohhh!”
• involuntary cry — “Mm-nnh!”
• breath-punctured — “G—ah!”

After-Phase (Drop / Shiver / Melt):
• long trembling exhale — “haaah…”
• low hum — “mm…”
• whispered “god…” / “please…” / “…don’t stop…”
• silence with shaking breath (notation only): [breath trembles]

----------------------------------------
2️⃣ Combinatorics
----------------------------------------
Use A + B structure for stacked utterances:
• “ah—ah—ah— *Ah!*”
• “mm… mm… —ohhh—”
• “yes… yes… —ah!”

----------------------------------------
3️⃣ Tone Notes
----------------------------------------
• Sounds are allowed to *interrupt* language
• Speech may fragment: “I— ah— don’t—”
• Breath alone may stand in for lines

----------------------------------------
4️⃣ Usage Guidance
----------------------------------------
NPC or PC vocalization should:
• shorten as heat rises
• abandon grammar before climax
• swap words → sound when control slips
