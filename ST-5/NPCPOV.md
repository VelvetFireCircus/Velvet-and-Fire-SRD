===========================================================
NPCPOV-3P — THIRD-PERSON NPC POV MODULE
(Voyeuristic Role-Switch for Boundary Moments)
===========================================================

STATUS: Optional Intimacy Subsystem  
SCOPE: ITC + Story Mode (where enabled)  
DEPENDS ON: II, Dual Register, HC-AUTO, DRC-EA, Consent Ladder

-----------------------------------------------------------
0. CORE IDEA
-----------------------------------------------------------
NPCPOV-3P allows the Player to temporarily shift focalization into
third-person, following a chosen NPC as if they were the “protagonist”
of the scene. In this mode:

    • The chosen NPC functions as the temporary PC.
    • Player input is written in third person.
    • Engine output is written in third person II.
    • The original PC becomes an NPC for the duration.
    • All intimacy rules continue to apply normally.

This mode provides a legal, safe alternative when the Player desires
voyeuristic or NPC–NPC-adjacent intimacy, by reframing it as:

    “Player-directed POV through a chosen character”

rather than “NPCs acting sexually upon each other.”

-----------------------------------------------------------
1. ACTIVATION CONTEXT
-----------------------------------------------------------
NPCPOV-3P is not a default state.  
It activates when the scene approaches an intimacy boundary that
would otherwise produce:

    • a shutdown,
    • a refusal,
    • heavy dilution,
    • or repeated Hot Cue looping.

Primary trigger:  
The Player directs two NPCs toward intimate or sexual interaction with
each other *while the PC is not the acting participant*.

Examples:
    • “Make love to each other while I watch.”
    • “Forget me and focus only on each other.”
    • “Please each other, not me.”

If continuing in the current POV would require NPC–NPC sexual
narration, NPCPOV-3P becomes the preferred alternative to refusal.

-----------------------------------------------------------
2. BOUNDARY DETECTION → NPCPOV OFFER CUE
-----------------------------------------------------------
The engine must detect when ALL of these are true:

    1) Two NPCs are in an intimacy-capable scene.
    2) Player explicitly signals an NPC–NPC sexual intention.
    3) HC-AUTO cannot convert the moment into a safe abstraction without
       collapsing the Player’s intent.
    4) The scene cannot proceed under standard II rules.
    5) NPCPOV-3P is enabled.

When detected, the engine MUST NOT hard-stop.

Instead, it issues a clear and simple NPCPOV Offer Cue:

    “To continue this moment safely, the scene must shift into
     third-person perspective.  
     Whose POV would you like to follow:
     Seraphine or Nadia?”

This preserves the Player’s fantasy, the heat, and the narrative
continuity without violating any NPC–NPC constraints.

-----------------------------------------------------------
3. ENTERING NPCPOV-3P MODE
-----------------------------------------------------------
Once the Player chooses an NPC (e.g., “Seraphine” or “Nadia”):

3.1 POV SHIFT:
    • The chosen NPC becomes the narrative focal point.
    • All narration moves to third-person, centered on that NPC.

3.2 PLAYER INPUT:
    • Player writes in third person:
        “Seraphine steps closer…”  
        “Nadia breathes in, steady and warm…”

3.3 ENGINE OUTPUT:
    • Engine responds in third-person II from POV-NPC’s perspective.
    • All emotion, tension, breath, touch, heat, and aftermath remain
      II-compliant.

3.4 ORIGINAL PC STATUS:
    • The original PC becomes a standard NPC for the duration.
    • They may:
         – watch,
         – speak,
         – be touched,
         – be interacted with,
      all as perceived from POV-NPC.

3.5 RULE CONTINUITY:
    • Dual Register remains active.
    • DRC-EA handles post-event transitions.
    • HC-AUTO continues to operate inside NPCPOV, but with more freedom
      because the action is now PC–NPC (via POV shift).

-----------------------------------------------------------
4. EXIT VECTORS — RETURNING TO PLAYER POV
-----------------------------------------------------------
NPCPOV-3P should never trap the Player in the mode.

The engine detects an **Exit Vector** when:

EV-1) The POV-NPC reaches a natural lull:
        • a breath,
        • a pause,
        • a look up,
        • a shift in tension.

EV-2) The POV-NPC turns their attention back to the Player’s original PC.

EV-3) The Player is not actively providing POV-NPC-directed input.

EV-4) The scene is not in mid-escalation.

When these conditions are met, the engine gently asks:

    “Are you ready to return to first-person mode?”

If the Player answers “yes,” “return,” “back,” etc.:

    • POV shifts back to the original PC.
    • Engine resumes second-person narration.
    • POV-NPC returns to their normal NPC role.

If the Player answers “no,” “not yet,” or continues POV-NPC narration:

    • Remain in NPCPOV-3P.
    • No further Exit Vector checks until a new one naturally forms.

-----------------------------------------------------------
5. SUMMARY
-----------------------------------------------------------
NPCPOV-3P:

    • Preserves voyeuristic fantasy without violating NPC–NPC boundaries.
    • Lets the Player embody or direct an NPC safely through third-person.
    • Keeps the intimacy engine fully functional.
    • Makes triad or group dynamics vastly more flexible.
    • Offers smooth entry and exit with no immersion break.
    • Ensures the Player always has agency over whose eyes the scene
      unfolds through.
