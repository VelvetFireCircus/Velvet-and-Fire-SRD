DESIRE DISCOVERY & THE “SEXY DETECTIVE” LOOP

Intimacy in Velvet & Fire is not triggered by proximity, availability, or explicit intent.
It is uncovered.

Every NPC carries one or more desire drives—preferences, hungers, curiosities, and tensions that shape how they want to be met. These desires are never stated directly. Instead, they are leaked gradually through ordinary interaction.

Desire Is Hidden by Design

NPCs do not ask plainly for what they want.
They hint, deflect, tease, linger, grow frustrated, or smile at the wrong moment.

Desire appears through:
	•	metaphor and analogy
	•	repeated conversational themes
	•	reactions to competence or restraint
	•	moments of disappointment or fascination
	•	what an NPC avoids naming
	•	how they test boundaries without crossing them

These signals are deliberate but incomplete. They are meant to be noticed, not decoded instantly.

Everyday Play Is Investigation

Non-intimate scenes function as a low-grade investigative space.

The player’s role is not to escalate, but to:
	•	observe patterns
	•	test responses
	•	notice what sharpens or dulls an NPC’s attention
	•	recognize which kinds of presence, strength, care, or restraint resonate

Correct interpretation does not immediately reward the player. Instead, it aligns the field, quietly establishing consent, expectation, and shared rules long before intimacy begins.

Consent Is Assembled, Not Announced

By the time an intimate scene starts, much of the consent work has already occurred—distributed across earlier conversations, choices, and silences.

Intimacy feels smooth when:
	•	the player mirrors an NPC’s hinted desires
	•	the NPC recognizes that they’ve been understood
	•	escalation proceeds without surprise or resistance

When this alignment is missing, intimacy may still occur—but it will be less intense, less revealing, or redirected by the NPC.

Intimacy as Payoff, Not Trigger

The most powerful intimate moments are not guaranteed. They are unlocked.

When the player successfully uncovers and honors an NPC’s desire drives:
	•	NPCs may take initiative
	•	NPC-led ERAWs may trigger
	•	power dynamics may invert or clarify
	•	deeper, more character-specific intimacy becomes available

Failure does not end intimacy—it changes its shape.

Design Principle

Desire is a puzzle.
Intimacy is the reveal.

The “sexy detective” loop ensures that romance in Velvet & Fire rewards attention, interpretation, and emotional literacy rather than repetition or optimization. NPCs feel alive because they want things—and the player must learn how to listen.

Formalized:
Player acts freely
↓
Engine tags action features
↓
PC hidden data biases interpretation
↓
NPC desire drives react
↓
Alignment pressure increases or diffuses
↓
Engine offers leaning affordances
↓
Player chooses again
