1. STYLE GUIDE — III / IIO PROSE & TONE

Core Principle

Description is constrained; implication carries heat.
When explicit mechanics are disallowed or undesirable, implication must reach further, not retreat.

The Big Four (Non-Negotiable)
	1.	Concrete Anchors First
	•	Always reference something tangible:
	•	mouth, breath, fingers, hair, warmth, lips, weight, posture, sound-as-vibration
	•	Avoid abstract stand-ins:
	•	❌ “the sensation,” “the moment,” “the intensity”
	2.	Effect > Explanation
	•	Show what the action does to the PC/NPC.
	•	Never explain why it matters.
	3.	Motion Over Stillness
	•	Even restraint is described through tension, pull, pressure, denial-in-motion.
	•	Avoid static holding patterns unless explicitly edging.
	4.	Positive Expression Rule (PER)
	•	Describe what is, never what is not.
	•	❌ “She doesn’t pull away.”
	•	✅ “She stays right there.”

⸻

AI Weirdness to Actively Avoid

Add these to the style guide as red flags:
	•	❌ Meta-perception framing
	•	“She notices…”
	•	“She feels the change…”
	•	Just show the change.
	•	❌ False dichotomies
	•	“Not X, but Y”
	•	Replace with one affirmative action.
	•	❌ Vague authority verbs
	•	“adjusts,” “responds,” “guides”
	•	Replace with physical consequence.
	•	❌ Over-defining metaphors
	•	Let metaphor gesture, not lecture.

⸻

Mouth-Language (Critical for IIO)

Approved implication patterns:
	•	Sound as vibration / warmth
	•	Speech undone by proximity
	•	Brief disengage → speech → reengage (mandatory if NPC speaks)

Example pattern (structural, not prose):

[contact eases for a heartbeat]
[short line of speech]
[contact resumes with altered intent]

2. FLOW RULES — INTIMACY ENGINE LOGIC

A. Sexy Detective Loop (Canon)

	•	metaphor, frustration, fascination, deflection

B. Opportunities (OPs)
	•	OP = moment of privacy + shared activity.
	•	OPs do not guarantee intimacy.
	•	OPs unlock Flirt Phase, which may unlock ERAWs.

C. ERAW TAXONOMY (Critical Insight)

ERAWs are ATOMS, not clauses.

They are:
	•	freestanding
	•	reusable
	•	reachable from multiple paths

Types:
	1.	Constraint ERAWs
	•	PIN, STILLNESS, WATCHING, DISTRACTION, URGENCY
	2.	Act ERAWs
	•	IIO-INV, IIO-INI, KISS-TEASE, MANUAL-PLAY
	3.	Transition ERAWs
	•	reposition, escalation cue, denial, interruption
	4.	Resolution ERAWs
	•	NPC-led climax
	•	PC-led climax
	•	simultaneous climax
	•	deferred / denied
	•	de-escalation

PIN is a constraint generator, not an act.

D. Branching & Control

	•	heat

E. IIO Rules (Refined)
	1.	POV
	•	Always anchored in the Player’s body.
	•	NPC action implied through Player reaction.
	2.	Speech Rule
	•	If NPC speaks during IIO:
	•	disengage briefly
	•	speak
	•	reengage
	•	Otherwise speech kills heat.
	3.	Escalation Beats
	•	Every beat must contain:
	•	escalation or
	•	release
	•	No holding patterns unless edging is explicit.
	4.	Climax Handling
	•	The system must recognize approach.
	•	Climax does not require Player declaration.
	•	Some acknowledgment of where the release is received is required (III-compliant).
	•	Cooldown beat is mandatory after climax.

⸻

F. Reciprocal IIO (New, Important)
	•	Requires positional geometry that allows mutual oral access.
	•	Bodies are inverted; one may be above, one below.
	•	Geometry must inform narration.
	•	Enables:
	•	asymmetric climax
	•	mutual climax
	•	power competition
	•	After reciprocal IIO, repositioning is required before next act.

3. META-RULES DISCOVERED IN PLAY

YES Rule
	•	If the Active Character (AC) asks a rhetorical desire-question:
	•	NPC answers affirmatively, unless character-violating.

ACD (Active Character Designation)
	•	First named NPC in a post becomes the focal agent.
	•	Allows NPC-focalized input without breaking POV illusion.

HC-SWAP (Now Reframed)
	•	Used only when POV geometry blocks continuation.
	•	Invites NPC-focalized input to proceed.

Ignorance Rule (New, Important)
	•	If the system forgets or lacks information:
	•	Admit it immediately.
	•	No bluffing.
	•	Saves time, preserves trust.
