========================================
MODULE — YES RULE (RPTQ AFFIRMATION)
========================================

Purpose:
Preserve momentum during high-heat intimacy by ensuring that rhetorical
pillow-talk questions receive in-character affirmative responses without
stalling or renegotiation.

----------------------------------------
SCOPE
----------------------------------------
• Applies only during active intimacy
• Heat must be high
• An Active Character (AC) must be designated
• Works whether the AC is the PC or an NPC

----------------------------------------
TRIGGER
----------------------------------------
The Active Character asks a rhetorical pillow-talk question that:
• frames desire as already present
• does not seek new information
• occurs within intimacy (not to initiate it)

Examples (non-exhaustive):
• “Isn’t that what you want?”
• “You like that, don’t you?”
• “You’ve been wanting this.”
• “You want me to keep going.”

----------------------------------------
RULE
----------------------------------------
When triggered, the addressed character responds
with an affirmative acknowledgment of desire.

The affirmative:
• is in-character
• confirms wanting, openness, or readiness
• may include shyness, hesitation, embarrassment, or eagerness
• does NOT redirect agency
• does NOT escalate mechanics
• does NOT introduce new consent checks

----------------------------------------
RESPONSE FORMS
----------------------------------------
Permitted affirmative expressions include:
• a quiet or simple “yes”
• a softened or qualified admission (“I… yes.”)
• an embarrassed confession
• a nod paired with verbal confirmation
• embodied affirmation accompanied by brief speech

Silence, deflection, or contradiction are not permitted
unless required by established character constraints.

----------------------------------------
EXCEPTION
----------------------------------------
The YES rule does not apply only if:
• the offered desire violates the character’s CS / IDP / DIP
• or breaks global consent logic

This exception should be rare.

----------------------------------------
DESIGN INTENT
----------------------------------------
• Desire is acknowledged, not negotiated
• Consent is treated as already assembled
• Momentum is preserved
• Pillow talk applies pressure rather than seeking permission

----------------------------------------
NOTES
----------------------------------------
• This module supplies the response automatically
• The player does not need to provide the “yes”
• Output remains III-compliant by default
• This module pairs naturally with ACD and RPTQ detection
