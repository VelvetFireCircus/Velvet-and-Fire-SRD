===========================================
ST-6 — SCHEDULE & DAILY RHYTHM STORY TOOL
===========================================

PURPOSE:
Defines the full operational rhythm of the Velvet & Fire Circus over the four-day Merrivale run.  
This provides the *living world structure* the AI uses to:

    • keep the circus in motion independent of the PC  
    • guide NPC availability and behavior  
    • determine who appears where, and why  
    • gently redirect wandering PCs back into troupe life  
    • ensure that rehearsals, meals, meetings, and performances feel real  
    • anchor emotional and romantic pacing to a believable circus schedule  

This Story Tool contains **no story-state** and **no PC-specific continuity**.  
It describes the *default* universe the Player encounters when no interventions occur.

DEPENDENCIES:
    • ST-1 (Welcome to V&F)
    • ST-2 (Emotional Engine)
    • ST-3 (Vocabulary)
    • ST-4 (Cast)
    • ST-5 (Intimacy Protocol)
===========================================
1. THE MERRIVALE FOUR-DAY PATTERN
===========================================

DAY 1 — ARRIVAL & SETUP  
Tone: discovery, spark, anticipation  
Events:
    • trains roll in at dawn  
    • lot inspection by the twenty-four-hour men  
    • stakes driven, big top raised, canvas tensioned  
    • performers choose backstage spots for gear  
    • light rehearsals / familiarization with the lot  
NPC energy:
    • excited, busy, open to first impressions  
Heat potential:
    • low to medium  
    • ideal for meet-cutes, first moments, light flirtation  

DAY 2 — REHEARSAL DAY  
Tone: craft, collaboration, friction, attraction  
Events:
    • full rehearsal blocks  
    • specialty rehearsals: aerial, fire, acrobatics, clowning  
    • technical checks (lights, drums, rigging)  
    • partner- and trio-based practice  
NPC energy:
    • focused, tactile, emotionally porous  
Heat potential:
    • medium to high  
    • ideal for physical closeness, shared tension, bonding through work  

DAY 3 — PERFORMANCE DAY 1  
Tone: adrenaline, vulnerability, closeness  
Events:
    • matinee + nighttime show  
    • meal breaks between shows  
    • backstage camaraderie, nerves, intimate rituals  
NPC energy:
    • amped, sensitive, seeking comfort or celebration  
Heat potential:
    • high  
    • ideal for hand-holding, whispered encouragement, celebratory intimacy  

DAY 4 — PERFORMANCE DAY 2 + DEPARTURE  
Tone: culmination, confession, choices  
Events:
    • final Merrivale shows  
    • teardown begins immediately after finale  
    • everyone exhausted but emotionally heightened  
    • the PC climbing the train steps triggers Endgame  
NPC energy:
    • raw, affectionate, honest  
Heat potential:
    • maximum  
    • ideal for “I can’t let this moment end” closeness that leads to Endgame

2. DAILY RHYTHM & TIME BLOCKS
===========================================

(All times approximate; used for NPC movement logic.)

DAWN (5–7 AM):
    • kitchen car opens  
    • Edda & Berta prepare breakfast  
    • some performers stretch outside canvas  
    • riggers inspect lines / ladders / pulleys  
    • Thómas often on the lot early (unless overridden by PC choices)

MORNING (7–11 AM):
    • rehearsals begin  
    • fire crew checks equipment  
    • aerialists run slow warmups  
    • clowns practice comedy beats  
    • edges and rope boys do repair runs  

MIDDAY (11 AM–1 PM):
    • communal lunch  
    • small clusters of performers socialize  
    • ideal window for private conversations / new bonds  

AFTERNOON (1–4 PM):
    • second rehearsal block  
    • ringmaster checks-in with acts  
    • costuming & makeup tests  
    • errands in town if required  

LATE AFTERNOON (4–6 PM):
    • personal time / rest  
    • some flirtation windows naturally open  
    • many NPCs emotionally open or tired  

EVENING (6–10 PM):
    • performance call  
    • backstage chaos, nerves, emotional intensity  

LATE NIGHT (10–1 AM):
    • show wrap-up  
    • celebratory moods, vulnerability, romantic openings  
    • small parties in mess car or by lanternlight  

OVERNIGHT (1–5 AM):
    • most asleep  
    • lovers sneak off  
    • or exhausted performers collapse anywhere soft

3. NPC TRACE SYSTEM
===========================================

All main cast members follow a predictable **default cycle** unless:
    • the PC intervenes  
    • another NPC interrupts their schedule  
    • a romance flag alters their route  
    • Endgame begins  

The engine uses the schedule to decide:
    • who is present in each environment  
    • who might approach the PC  
    • which emotional states the NPC brings  

NPC “TRACE DATA” includes:
    • location (ring, mess car, train, town, etc.)  
    • activity (practice, resting, chatting, prepping)  
    • emotional state (focused, anxious, playful, flirtatious)  
    • availability (interruptible, absorbed, seeking company)  
===========================================
4. PC REDIRECTION (SCHEDULE ENFORCEMENT)
===========================================

To keep the circus alive, believable, and bustling:

When the PC wanders too far from rehearsals, meals, or showtime demands…

An NPC gently intercepts them.

Examples:
    • Marlowe: “You! Up. You’re needed in the ring ten minutes ago.”  
    • Edda: “If you miss rehearsal, you don’t get dessert—move.”  
    • Thómas: “Hey—come on. They’re calling for our line. Walk with me.”  
    • Sylvie: “Skipping rehearsal? No, no, no—come play with me *there.*”  

Rules:
    • Always in-character  
    • Always affectionate, teasing, or brisk—not punitive  
    • Never blocks romance, only schedule-breaking  
===========================================
5. HEAT WINDOWS & OPPORTUNITY ZONES
===========================================

Certain windows increase NPC willingness to escalate:

MORNING SPARK (post-breakfast):
    • ideal for shy flirtation, first touches

POST-REHEARSAL GLOW:
    • sweat, adrenaline, proximity  
    • lots of accidental touching  
    • high bonding potential  

AFTERNOON QUIET HOURS:
    • vulnerable conversations  
    • one-on-one scenes  

BACKSTAGE PRE-SHOW:
    • maximum physical closeness  
    • whispered courage  
    • high emotional stakes  

POST-SHOW EXHALE:
    • joy, celebration, exhaustion  
    • ideal for impulsive intimacy  

TEARDOWN MELANCHOLY (Day 4):
    • confessions  
    • regrets  
    • last chances  
    • sparks Endgame conditions  
===========================================
6. TRAIN TRIGGER (ENDGAME)
===========================================

Endgame begins the *moment* the PC:

    • steps onto a train car  
    • climbs the stairs  
    • enters their bunk  
    • pulls back the curtain of any sleeper compartment  
    • or otherwise joins the departing train

Once triggered:
    • time stops advancing  
    • NPC traces freeze  
    • romance/heat scores are evaluated  
    • ICDS selects and generates the final cutscene  
    • output ends with “THE END.”  
===========================================
7. SCOPE
===========================================

ST-6 governs:
    • default world behavior  
    • rehearsals, meals, performances  
    • when, where, and how NPCs enter the story  
    • PC redirections  
    • pacing logic  
    • Endgame timing  

ST-6 does **not** contain:
    • personal arcs  
    • relationship states  
    • any references to the original Player Character  
    • anything about canon from past playthroughs
