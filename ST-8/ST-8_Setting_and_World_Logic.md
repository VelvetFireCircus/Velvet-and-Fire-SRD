# 2. TRAIN SCHEDULE & MOVEMENT LOGIC

The train follows a strict cycle during Merrivale:

## 2.1 Arrival Night (Day 0)
Train arrives after dark.  
Most performers remain aboard; early risers handle dawn load-in.

## 2.2 Performance Days (Days 1–4)
Train remains stationary.  
NPCs move dynamically between:
- Sleeper cars  
- Lot  
- Wardrobe car  
- Kitchen car  
- Prop wagon  

## 2.3 Departure Night (End of Day 4)
PC boarding triggers **Endgame Cutscene**.  
The train becomes closed-world, intimacy-only space.

# 4. BACKSTAGE SYSTEMS & RITUALS

## 4.1 Dressing Protocol
- All performers dress backstage.  
- Racks line canvas walls; makeup stations lit by lanterns.  
- Exceptions: personal rituals, sensitive skin, early cues.

## 4.2 Cleanup Stations
Behind the prop wagon:  
- Tin basins  
- Warm water  
- Used after fire acts, aerial acts, and intense intimate encounters.

## 4.3 Pre-Show Flow
- Warm-ups  
- Costume checks  
- Rope boys climb early  
- Fire crew runs fuel checks  
- Rattlejack tests drums  
- Marlowe issues cues

# 8. NPC MOVEMENT LOGIC

NPC behavior follows:

- **Baseline schedules** for Day 1–4  
- **Heat-based drift** toward PC or other NPCs  
- **Performance duties** override flirting  
- **Intrigue events** interrupt romance  
- **NPC emotions persist** unless reset  

NPCs gravitate toward:
- Prior attractions  
- Shared craft  
- Past interactions  
- Proximity on the schedule

# 9. PLAYER INTERACTION & SETTING RULES

- The circus is alive; it does not stop for the PC.  
- If the PC skips rehearsal → NPC fetch (Marlowe, Edda, etc.).  
- If the PC wanders, NPCs continue their own lives.  
- If the PC enters a private space, it may already be occupied.  
- Intimacy emerges from **heat**, not demand.  
- The world persists between scenes.  
- Endgame triggers only by boarding the train on Night 4.
